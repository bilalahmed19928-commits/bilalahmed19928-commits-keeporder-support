KeepOrder

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

KeepOrder helps you organize and remember where your household items are stored. Your privacy is important to us.

Data Collection

KeepOrder does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information to the developer.

Information Stored on Your Device

Information you add to KeepOrder, including photos, item names, box numbers, locations, categories, and notes, is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's organization features.

Camera and Photo Library

KeepOrder may request permission to access your camera or photo library so you can add photos to your saved memories. These permissions are used only to provide features you request.

Third-Party Sharing

KeepOrder does not sell or share your personal information with advertisers.

Children's Privacy

KeepOrder does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as KeepOrder develops. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please contact the developer through the KeepOrder support page.
