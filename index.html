KeepOrder Support

Welcome to KeepOrder Support. KeepOrder helps you remember exactly where you stored your household items.

How to Use KeepOrder

Add a photo of an item, give it a name, enter its box number or location, and select a category. You can then quickly find your saved items later using search and categories.

Managing Your Memories

You can view, edit, favorite, or delete your saved memories. KeepOrder also allows you to organize items into categories such as Clothes, Kids, Kitchen, Documents, Decorations, Storage Boxes, and more.

Your Data

KeepOrder is designed to keep your saved memories on your device. Deleting the app may remove locally stored information, so make sure important information is saved elsewhere when needed.

Need Help?

If you experience a problem with KeepOrder or have feedback, please contact the KeepOrder support team.
